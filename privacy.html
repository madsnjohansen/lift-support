Lift Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-11-14

Lift is local-first. It does not create an account, collect analytics, send data to the developer, or request network access.

Data stored on the watch

Lift stores workout drafts, up to 50 completed workouts, previous-set data, favorites, custom exercises, rest duration, and vibration preference in Garmin's Connect IQ application storage.

When activity recording is available, Lift creates a Garmin strength activity. Garmin may record duration, heart rate, laps, and other standard activity data. Lift adds custom FIT developer fields for repetitions, weight, exercise name, set number, set volume, total sets, total volume, and exercise count.

Data sharing

Lift does not transmit data to the developer. Watch-to-Garmin-Connect syncing is handled under Garmin's terms and privacy policy. Garmin Connect may or may not display developer fields as native strength exercises or sets.

Retention and deletion

Lift-owned data remains on the watch until the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared. Garmin activities already saved to the watch or synced to Garmin Connect are separate and remain until deleted through Garmin's activity controls.
