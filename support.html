Lift Support

Lift is a local-first strength-training watch app for Garmin Venu 4 41 mm and 45 mm.

Contact

Email: liftstrengthlog@gmail.com. Support is provided on a best-effort basis.

Please include the watch model and size, Lift version, reproduction steps, and a screenshot or Connect IQ crash log when available.

Data and activity recording

Lift stores workouts and preferences locally on the watch. Removing the app removes Lift-owned data. Activities already saved to Garmin Connect must be deleted through Garmin's activity controls.

Garmin Connect does not guarantee that Lift's custom FIT developer fields appear as native strength exercises or sets.
